Masmaak: listen to the Holy Quran

A free Quran audio app with 200+ reciters: full mushaf recitations, selected recitations, Ramadan duas, ruqyah and adhkar, offline downloads and ayah tracking. No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking.

Recitations are streamed from mp3quran.net with permission; Quran text from the Tanzil Project.
